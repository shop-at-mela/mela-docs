# Mela Reel Audio Strategy

**Last updated:** August 16, 2026
**Scope:** How Instagram reel audio drives (or blocks) brand awareness, the Business-account music constraint that specifically affects `@shopatmela`, and Mela's recommended audio stack + trending-sound tactics. Feeds the manual "add audio at publish" step in every reel (`hook-engineering.md` §4, `feedback_blotato_reel_production`).

> This is a strategy brief, not legal advice. The licensing points below are widely reported by social-marketing + IP sources (see Sources); confirm specifics with counsel before betting a campaign on a particular track.

---

## TL;DR

1. **Audio is a ranking + distribution lever, not decoration.** Reels play sound-on by default; IG's algorithm favors trending/licensed audio and gives every sound its own **audio discovery feed** (an extra way to be found). Sound-on creative measurably out-engages silent.
2. **The catch for Mela:** `@shopatmela` is a **Business account**, so it is **locked out of the full trending-song library** — it can only use the **Meta Sound Collection** (~14,000 royalty-free, commercially-cleared tracks) or independently licensed music. The recognizable pop songs your competitors-on-Creator-accounts use are not legally available to a brand promoting products.
3. **Mela's default:** build reach on **Meta Sound Collection + a licensed library (Epidemic Sound / Artlist) + an original/"signature" Mela sound** — *not* the Creator-account trending-song loophole. Ride genuinely-cleared trending sounds when one fits, using the timing tactics in §5.

## 1. Why audio is a growth lever

- **Ranking signal.** IG's Reels algorithm treats the audio track as a strong signal; reels on trending/licensed audio get surfaced more.
- **A second distribution channel.** Every sound has its own feed ("X reels use this audio"). A reel tied to a rising sound can be discovered by people browsing that sound, not just your followers — pure top-of-funnel reach, which is exactly Mela's cold-start need.
- **Sound-on by default.** Unlike Stories, Reels play with sound on. Audio (music, voiceover, ambient) is a *primary* creative element — it can carry the hook so the visual doesn't have to fight text overlays (which is also why we keep on-screen text minimal, `feedback_blotato_reel_production`).
- **Timing tail.** A strong reel keeps surfacing for weeks/months; audio choice compounds over that tail.

## 2. The Business-account constraint (the gotcha)

- Meta's licensing deals with record labels cover **personal / Creator, non-commercial** use only. A **Business** account is treated as commercial, so IG restricts it to the **Meta Sound Collection** (~14k royalty-free tracks cleared for commercial use) or music you've licensed yourself.
- **"It's in the picker" ≠ "cleared for commercial use."** Some Business accounts still *see* popular tracks in the library; using them for brand content is the trap — the underlying license doesn't cover commercial promotion, and enforcement (mutes, takedowns, in rare cases claims) is inconsistent, not permission.
- **Practical symptom:** open the Reels audio picker on `@shopatmela` and you'll mostly see unfamiliar instrumental tracks while creator accounts see Sabrina Carpenter et al. That's account classification, not a bug.

## 3. The Creator-account question — should Mela switch?

Switching Business → Creator unlocks the full trending-song library and keeps most tools (insights, etc.). Many small brands do it. **Recommendation: don't, for Mela.**

- **Copyright follows the *use*, not the account label.** A brand promoting products is commercial use regardless of whether the profile says "Creator." The loophole changes what the picker *shows*, not whether the use is licensed.
- **Risk posture.** Mela is a pre-revenue discovery brand in warmup; a music claim or a muted/removed reel on a key post is downside we don't need, and the founder runs a deliberately low-legal-risk operation.
- **Better long game.** An **original/signature sound** builds *audio recognition* over time and enables UGC (shoppers recreating your format with your sound) — a moat a borrowed trending track never gives.

Revisit only if a specific, cleared campaign makes the tradeoff worth it — and decide it deliberately, not by drifting into the loophole.

## 4. Mela's recommended audio stack

In priority order:
1. **Meta Sound Collection first** — free, in-app, commercially cleared. Filter by mood (calm, warm, editorial) to match the Mela aesthetic. Zero legal risk.
2. **A licensed library** (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Soundstripe) — much larger, pre-cleared for social commercial use, better-quality tracks that still won't be "recognizable." Worth a subscription once reel volume is steady (we're now at 4–5/week).
3. **An original / signature Mela sound** — a short motif or a lightly-branded audio bed reused across a defined *series* (e.g. the "discovered on Mela" reveal). Builds recognition + is UGC-able. Owned outright, so no license question ever.
4. **Genuinely-cleared trending sounds** — when a rising sound is in the commercial library and *fits*, ride it per §5. Don't force-fit a trend that fights the calm brand voice (`hook-engineering.md` §2 "Never").

## 5. Trending-sound tactics (when a cleared one fits)

- **Find it:** the ↗ arrow next to a sound = gaining traction. Tap any sound to see its use-count. A sound recurring across your niche is trending.
- **Timing sweet spot:** a rising sound with **~5k–30k uses** is early-adoption gold; past **~100k** it's usually past peak (some evergreen sounds plateau and keep working — check whether the count is still climbing). Act within **24–48h** of spotting it.
- **Cross-platform arbitrage:** sounds often trend on TikTok before Instagram — spotting one early on TikTok and posting to IG ahead of its IG peak gets you into the audio feed before saturation.
- **Balance:** keep trending audio *under* any voiceover/educational narration at low volume — momentum without compromising the calm voice.
- **Don't over-reuse one sound:** repeatedly using the identical track can read as low creative diversity and dampen distribution. (Resolve the tension with §4.3: reserve the *signature* sound for a defined series; vary elsewhere.)

## 6. How it plugs into the Mela workflow

- Audio is added at the **manual IG publish step** (Blotato clips are silent). Same step where the founder adds the one-line hook overlay.
- **Per reel:** pick a Meta-Sound-Collection / licensed track whose mood matches the tile's persona + payoff (e.g. warm/editorial for a product reveal; a cleaner utility bed for the cert-decode education reel).
- **Log the sound used** alongside the post so the monthly teardown (`hook-engineering.md` §6) can see which audio correlates with reach/watch-time — audio becomes a tracked variable, not a guess.

## 7. Open decisions for the founder

- [ ] **Stay Business (recommended) vs. switch to Creator** for the trending library — decide deliberately (see §3).
- [ ] **Subscribe to a licensed library** (Epidemic Sound / Artlist, ~$10–25/mo) now that reels are 4–5/week? Unlocks far better tracks, fully cleared.
- [ ] **Commission a signature Mela sound / motif** for the reveal series (small one-time cost, compounding brand-recognition + UGC upside).
