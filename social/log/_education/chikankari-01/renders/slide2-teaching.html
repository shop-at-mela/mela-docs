How to spot the real thing

Chikankari, by hand not by print

1

Real hand work feels soft, part of the fabric. A print sits flat on top.

2

Flip it over. Hand embroidery leaves a clean, threaded back. Print leaves nothing.

3

Look for slight irregularity stitch to stitch. Machine perfect is too perfect.

4

Jali, the fine net stitch, has no machine equivalent. If you see it, it's hand done.
