How a jutti is made

A jutti takes a human hand, start to finish

1

The upper is cut and shaped by hand to the jutti's flat, curled-toe silhouette.

2

Embellishment goes on next: Kantha running-stitch and silver thread, worked one pass at a time.

3

The sole is hand-stitched to the upper, then checked by eye. Mumbai's karigars do this for a living, now.
