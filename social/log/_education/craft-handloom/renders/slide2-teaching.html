How it's made

Handloom cloth, thread by thread

1

Warp threads are strung onto the loom one by one, by hand, before weaving starts.

2

A weaver throws the shuttle back and forth, pressing each thread into place.

3

A pattern or border is built row by row, no computer guiding the next pass.

4

One weaver finishes about one yard a day. Small unevenness in the weave is the human hand, not a flaw.
