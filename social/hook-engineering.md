# Mela Hook Engineering & Short-Form Playbook

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026
**Canonical home for:** emotional-trigger taxonomy, hook mechanics (first 2–3s / first caption line / slide 1), the short-form script skeleton, the monthly top-performer teardown, Mela's 5 content angles, and the repeatable AI content workflow. Rules that already have a home stay there — this doc **references** them, never restates: diaspora/situation hooks + education visual system + caption style → `visual-style-guide.md`; routing/cadence/grid/rotation → `category-routing.yaml`; personas → `buyer-personas.md`; education queue → `education-topics.yaml`; the platform "why" → `social-content-strategy-prd.md`.

> **Voice guardrail (read first).** Mela adapts the modern short-form playbook to a **calm, non-exoticizing** brand. We engineer hooks hard, but we do **not** manufacture controversy, bait fear, or clickbait a payoff we can't honor. Every trigger below is translated into a Mela-safe form. If a hook only works by othering Indian culture, inventing conflict, over-promising, or **putting down the shopper's own local / family-run desi store** (belonging ≠ gatekeeping), it's a defect — see `visual-style-guide.md` → What NOT to Do.

---

## 1. Why this exists

A cold account lives or dies in the **first 2–3 seconds** (reel) or the **first ~125 caption characters** (feed/Pinterest — the sliver everyone sees before "more"). Our visuals are already strong; the gap is the *hook* — the reason to stop scrolling before the craft even registers. This doc makes the hook a designed object, not an afterthought, and gives the operator a repeatable loop for finding what's working in the niche right now.

This is a **short-form-primary, hooks-everywhere** strategy (updated 2026-08-14 after an expert-panel critique reversed the earlier ~2/week stance — see Superseded). **Short-form video is now the primary reach format at 4–5 reels/week;** carousels and stills are *supporting*. Reels carry cold-start distribution, so hook engineering is applied hardest there — and to every other format's opening moment too. Production leans on Blotato semi-automation and accepts rough-but-fast over polished-but-rare (volume is how a hook system learns).

## 2. The emotional-trigger taxonomy (Mela-safe)

The niche playbook names six scroll-stopping triggers: **surprise, fear, ego, urgency, desire, curiosity.** Used raw they fight Mela's voice. Here is each one translated, with the Mela-safe form we actually ship and the line we never cross.

| Raw trigger | Mela-safe form | Example opener | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Surprise** | The counterintuitive craft fact | "No two are ever identical. Here's why." / "This took 3 days to print. By hand." | Shock for its own sake |
| **Fear** → **Loss aversion** | The thing you'll miss / how to buy well | "The good Diwali china never survives the suitcase from India." / "Here's how to spot true hand embroidery before you buy." | Dread, alarm, FOMO baiting, **and disparaging the shopper's own local / family-run desi store** |
| **Ego** → **Taste / insider knowledge** | You'll know what most people can't spot | "Know real hand embroidery before you buy." / "The piece your guests will ask about." | Superiority, shaming the viewer |
| **Urgency** → **Genuine occasion / restock** | A real date or a real supply gap | "Diwali is three weeks out and this ships from India in about two." | Fake countdowns, invented scarcity |
| **Desire** | The payoff you actually want | "The festive table you keep scrolling past — here's where it's from." | Unattainable/over-styled aspiration |
| **Curiosity** | An open loop the payoff closes | "There's a reason this costs what it does. It's not the gold." | Loops the post never resolves (bait) |
| **Belonging** *(Mela-added)* | The diaspora *situation*, never identity | "You used to wait for your mom's next visit to restock the good baby oil." | Naming the viewer's ethnicity — see Diaspora Anchoring |

**Belonging is our highest-yield trigger** because it's the one competitors outside the diaspora can't copy. It's the same principle as `visual-style-guide.md` → Diaspora Anchoring: **anchor the shopper's *situation*, never their *identity*.** Reach for it first; layer surprise/curiosity/loss on top.

## 3. Hook mechanics (where the hook lives)

The hook is not "the caption." It's the **first thing consumed in the format**, and most people never read the caption body (`visual-style-guide.md` → "Where the anchor must live"). Put the engineered line in the layer people actually see, in this priority:

1. **The visual / scene** (~90% of the impression) — for product photos there's no text, so the **scene is the hook**: stage the US context (a Diwali table in an American home) so the situation lands before a word is read.
2. **On-image / on-screen text** (reels + education hook cards — the formats where text is allowed): reel first frame, the hook-card slide 1. This is where the §2 line goes verbatim.
3. **First caption line (~125 chars)** — the one caption element everyone sees. Lead with the hook; never bury it in paragraph two.
4. **Caption body + Pinterest description + alt text** — reinforcement + SEO for the minority who expand.

**The 3-second reel rule.** By second 3 the viewer must have (a) a stopped scroll, (b) an open loop or a stated stakes, and (c) a reason it's *for them*. Second 0–1 = visual pattern-break; second 1–3 = the on-screen hook line.

## 4. The short-form script skeleton

For any short-form video (the ~2/week teaser reels, or a scripted reel when footage exists). Keep it **under 30 seconds** and optimize the structure for **watch-through, replays, comments, and shares** — not just views.

```
[0:00–0:02]  HOOK        Pattern-break visual + the §2 line. Aggressive on curiosity/
                         stakes, calm in tone. State the loop or the stakes now.
[0:02–0:20]  PAYOFF      Deliver fast. Answer the loop, show the craft/contrast/reveal.
                         One idea. Tight cuts. Earn the replay (a detail worth a second look).
[0:20–0:28]  RESOLVE     Land the satisfying beat — the "oh, that's why" or the finished scene.
[0:28–0:30]  SUBTLE CTA  Soft, discovery-framed. "Discovered on Mela." / "Full brand on Mela."
                         Never a hard sell; never "available from Mela" (we curate, brands ship).
```

**Engagement levers to build in (not bolt on):**
- **Replays** — put one detail early that rewards a second watch (a stitch, a reveal you only catch on loop).
- **Comments** — end on a gentle either/or or a "could you spot it?" that invites a reply, never rage-bait.
- **Shares** — the belonging/loss hooks ("the good stuff never survives the suitcase") are the ones a diaspora viewer sends to family. Write *for the send*.
- **Watch-time** — no throat-clearing. The first frame is already the hook; no "hi guys" intro.

Audio + final publish for reels remain a **manual in-IG step** (Blotato templates are silent) — `category-routing.yaml` → `grid.reel_production_note`.

**Reel types now in weekly rotation (target 4–5/week).** All three obey the skeleton above — **the payoff beat is mandatory for every one of them:**
- **Product reel** — a real product moving through a scene, resolving on the detail/finished-look payoff. Never AI-generate the product (`visual-style-guide.md`).
- **How-it's-made / education reel** — process → reveal, from an `education-topics.yaml` topic. Slide-1/first-frame is a hook, never a product (Education Visual System).
- **Row-anchor teaser** — the brand/theme collage. **It must now land a payoff** (a reveal, a finished-scene beat, one product detail) before converging into the wordmark. A pure wordmark-converge with nothing to resolve is a **defect** — it violates the skeleton and won't retain past 2 seconds.

## 5. Mela's 5 content angles

Prompt-derived deliverable: the **5 strongest content angles optimized for AI-generated content + short-form.** These are the recurring "shapes" every post should slot into. Each maps to a trigger, a persona, and an existing content type so nothing is invented from scratch. **This list is refreshed monthly by the teardown in §6.**

| # | Angle | Core trigger | Lead persona | Format home | Hook seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Belonging / the suitcase economy** (restock-from-India, ships to your door) | Belonging + relief | Neha | Product reel/scene + situation caption | "You used to wait for a family visit to restock this. Not anymore." |
| 2 | **Discovery — the brands you can't find** (the curator wedge, as a hook) | Curiosity + desire | Neha / Priya | Product/collection reel or carousel | "The brand behind your favorite kurta doesn't ship to the US. This one does." |
| 3 | **How it's actually made** (living-culture craft teardown) | Curiosity | Arun | Education (`cultural_education`) reel/carousel | "Three days. One scarf. Here's every step." |
| 4 | **What the claim really means** (certification / "handmade" / "sustainable" decoded) | Taste + trust | Sarah | Education (`trust_service`) myth-vs-fact | "'Handmade' is unregulated. Here's what to actually check." |
| 5 | **Occasion-in-America** (host the festival far from where it's made) | Desire + genuine urgency | Priya | Product/gift-guide carousel + occasion caption | "Hosting your first Diwali away from home? Start with the table." |

The old standalone "real vs. lookalike / spot-the-fake" angle is **dropped** (2026-08-14): it disparaged the audience's own desi retail. The useful part — *helping a shopper recognize genuine hand-craft* — survives as an **empowering treatment inside #3 and #4** ("here's how the real thing is made / what to check"), never as a gotcha headline and never by naming a shop as a seller of fakes.

**Coverage rule:** across a batch, vary the angle so all four personas get served. The Aug 2026 panel found we over-index on angle #3 (Arun's craft-heritage lane) and under-serve #4 (Sarah/certs) and #1 (Neha/familiar-brands + price + belonging). Rotate deliberately — see `visual-style-guide.md` → Diaspora Anchoring "Coverage watch-out."

## 6. The monthly top-performer teardown

Prompts #1 and #3 in one loop. Run **monthly** (not daily — daily trend-chasing fights warmup + the verify-before-caption rule). The operator runs it; accounts stay in warmup so this is research, not engagement.

**Inputs.** The highest-performing **Instagram Reels, TikToks, and Reddit posts** from the last 30 days in Mela's niche — Indian craft, diaspora shopping, festival hosting, ethical/handmade goods, the adjacent creators. Use `firecrawl_search` / web search + manual scan; Reddit surface = `reddit-strategy.md` subreddits.

**Also mine our own search data (new input, 2026-08-17).** The ingestion pipeline's **diaspora synonym map** (`search_synonyms`) and the on-site **zero-result query logs** (`search-ranking-relevance-prd.md` §4B/§7) are a first-party demand + language signal the niche scan can't give you: the *actual* US↔Indian words shoppers use, and the things they searched for and didn't find. The vocabulary gap itself is a belonging hook (see §7); the unmet searches are angle-#2 (discovery) and angle-#5 (occasion) fuel. This is the same loop `reddit-strategy.md` feeds — one demand signal, three surfaces.

**Extract (the prompt #1 teardown):**
- Repeating **hooks** — the exact first lines / first frames that recur.
- **Visual styles & formats** — carousel vs. reel, talking-head vs. b-roll, text-on-screen conventions.
- **Emotional triggers** — which of §2 are doing the work.
- What consistently earns **saves, shares, and comments** (not just views).

**Break down the hook (the prompt #3 pass):** for the top 3–5 hooks, dissect structure, pacing, and the trigger used in the **first 3 seconds**. Then generate **5 new hook variations** that are more curiosity-driven and situation-anchored — run them through the §2 Mela-safe filter before any reach the swipe file.

### Promote / kill criteria (what makes a hook or angle "win")

The teardown is **data-driven, not editorial** — it scores *our own* posts against the account's rolling baseline, not gut feel. Metrics come from the Blotato dashboard + IG Insights (engagement side) and GA4 by `utm_campaign` (click-through side); definitions + the scoring table live in `metrics-log.md`. Thresholds are **relative to baseline** because absolute numbers are meaningless on a warmup account:

| Signal | Promote a hypothesis → *proven* | Kill / retire |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach / impressions** vs. trailing-30-day median | ≥ 1.5× median across ≥ 2 posts | ≤ 0.6× median across ≥ 3 posts |
| **3-second view rate** (reels) | Top third of reels that month | Bottom third, 2 months running |
| **Save + share rate** (the real intent signal) | Above account median | Consistently below |
| **Profile taps / link CTR** (GA4 `utm_campaign` session) | Any angle that drives sessions | Zero sessions after ≥ 3 fair tries |

Rules: a swipe-file line stays a **hypothesis** until it clears a promote row on real posts; an **angle** is retired (or reframed) if it hits a kill row across a fair sample. "Fair sample" = enough posts that it isn't one bad slot/time. **No data yet = don't promote anything** — until the account has a baseline (a few weeks of posts), §7 stays entirely hypotheses and the teardown is directional only.

**Outputs (write them down):**
1. Refresh **§5's 5 content angles** if the niche has moved or an angle hit a kill row.
2. **Promote** hooks that cleared a promote row into the proven set, **retire** killed ones, and add new §2-safe variations as fresh hypotheses in the **swipe file** (§7).
3. Note format shifts for `/social-launch` Phase 1 selection.

Log the teardown date + the refreshed angles + promote/kill decisions in `metrics-log.md` so the monthly cadence and the scoring are visible.

The monthly teardown (§6) promotes a line to *proven* only after it clears the §6 thresholds on real posts, and kills the rest. Lead with belonging (the moat); vary triggers across a batch.

Favor these when scripting reels this batch. The **vocabulary-bridge** lines above are a *distinct* structure — recognition/naming, not a numbered loop — and are the one hook family that doubles as an ad for the diaspora search itself.

Every entry must survive the §2 "Never" column (including the no-disparaging-desi-retail rule) and the `visual-style-guide.md` What-NOT-to-Do list.

## 8. The repeatable AI content workflow (prompt #4, mapped to Mela)

Prompt #4 asks for a complete AI pipeline: trending topics → high-retention scripts → matching AI visuals → short-form videos → optimized captions/hashtags, as a repeatable daily loop. Mela already **has** this pipeline split across two skills and the canonical docs — this table names each stage, its Mela owner, and its guardrail so nothing is re-derived. It is **skill-triggered (assisted batch), not event-driven daily** until the Sharetribe→Blotato watcher exists (`social-content-strategy-prd.md` → Automation).

| Prompt #4 stage | Mela owner | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| **Identify trending topics** | Monthly teardown (§6) + `education-topics.yaml` queue + seasonal calendar (`accounts.md`) — **not** daily trend-chasing | Every craft/cert claim verified on the live listing before it reaches copy (`/social-launch` Phase 1) |
| **Generate high-retention scripts** | §4 script skeleton + §2 trigger + §7 swipe file | Under 30s; calm voice; subtle discovery CTA |
| **Create matching AI visuals** | `visual-style-guide.md` → Tool decision procedure (real product photo / Blotato illustrative / HTML card) | Never AI-generate a *product*; exact text via HTML, not a Blotato layer; watermark in the HTML/SVG layer |
| **Turn into short-form video** | Blotato reels (product / how-it's-made / anchor), **4–5/week — primary reach** | Every reel lands a payoff (§4); audio + publish are a **manual in-IG** step; fall back to static card only if a reel truly can't be made |
| **Optimize captions + hashtags** | Caption structure → `visual-style-guide.md`; hashtags → §9 (IG) / keywords not hashtags → `pinterest-playbook.md` | First caption line carries the hook; discovery framing; no price where markup is high |
| **Repeatable loop** | `/social-launch` (weekly batch of 9) + `/social-review` (single post) + monthly teardown | Wife review on grid shape (when grid on); UTM + alt text + watermark on every asset |

**Cadence summary:** *monthly* teardown → refreshes angles + swipe file. *Weekly* `/social-launch` batch → applies them, producing **4–5 short-form reels** (primary reach) plus supporting carousels/stills. *Per-post* `/social-review` → engineers the individual hook. Still not a *daily* firehose (warmup + verify-before-caption), but short-form volume is deliberately higher than before so the hook system gets enough reps to learn. **Budget note:** 4–5 reels/week runs above the ≤3 hr/week creation target in `social-content-strategy-prd.md`; that PRD lags this decision and should be revisited — until then, lean on Blotato semi-automation and accept rough-but-fast.

## 9. Instagram Reel / feed hashtags (brief)

Pinterest uses **keywords, not hashtags** — see `pinterest-playbook.md` §4. For **Instagram** only:
- **Max 5 hashtags — Blotato's API hard-rejects a post over 5** (confirmed 2026-08-27: all 4 posts in that batch failed at schedule time with 7). Mix **niche + mid + situational**: craft/technique (`#chikankari` `#blockprint`), discovery/occasion (`#diwaligifts` `#indianwedding`), audience-situation (`#desiabroad` `#indianfoodabroad`) — **situation, never an ethnic-identity label on the viewer.**
- Put them **in the caption end or first comment**, not on the image.
- Skip generic mega-tags (`#love` `#instagood`) — zero targeting value.
- Reuse a small vetted set per angle rather than a fresh 30 each time.

## Superseded / prior stance

Before 2026-08-13, hook discipline lived implicitly inside `visual-style-guide.md` → Diaspora Anchoring (the "situation hook" idea) with no trigger taxonomy, no script skeleton, and no teardown loop, and Reels were treated purely as the semi-automated row anchor. That guidance is still correct and is now the *situation/belonging* row of §2 — this doc generalizes it into a full hook system and adds the monthly teardown + AI-workflow map.

**2026-08-14 — expert-panel revisions (this version).** A five-expert critique reversed three of the original stances:
- **Reels ~2/week → 4–5/week, short-form-primary.** *Why:* short-form is the cold-start reach lever and the ~2/week teaser contradicted §4's payoff rule. The prior "carousel-first, reels ~2/week" stance is superseded; carousels are now supporting. (Trade-off accepted: runs above the PRD's ≤3 hr/week budget — see §8.) **↳ ITSELF SUPERSEDED 2026-09-05 (see next).**
- **Short-form-primary → multi-product carousels primary (2026-09-05, founder preference).** *Why:* the founder prefers Blotato-improved multi-product collection carousels (the early Fizzy Goblet / ChooseKind format) over single-product reels, whose current Ken-Burns creation method underperformed on taste. Reels are demoted to the exception **until a better reel-creation method is found** — not abandoned. Carousels are the IG default again; operative config in `category-routing.yaml` → cadence. See `[[feedback_ig_multiproduct_carousel_default]]`.
- **"Real vs. lookalike / spot-the-fake" headline angle → dropped.** *Why:* it disparaged the audience's own (often family-run) desi retail — gatekeeping wearing a belonging label. Reframed to empowering "recognize real hand-craft" as a *treatment*, belonging promoted to angle #1, discovery added as a hook angle (§5).
- **Swipe file "tested well" → untested hypotheses + measurement layer.** *Why:* nothing has been tested pre-launch; §6 now carries promote/kill criteria so the teardown is data-driven, not editorial.

Deliberate stances **kept** (not overridden): calm non-exoticizing voice, skill-triggered (not daily-automated) production, verify-before-caption, belonging as the moat. Deferred to backlog: a community/engagement layer (in-voice replies, save/share prompts, UGC/creator seeding) — raised by the panel, not yet built.

**Reel audio strategy (done 2026-08-16 → `reel-audio-strategy.md`).** Best-practices pass on how brands use reel audio for awareness: audio as a ranking + discovery-feed lever, the **Business-account music constraint** (`@shopatmela` is Business → Meta Sound Collection ~14k cleared tracks only; full trending library = Personal/Creator, and copyright follows the *use* not the account label, so the Creator loophole isn't recommended), Mela's recommended stack (Meta Sound Collection + licensed library + original signature sound), trending-sound timing (~5k–30k-use sweet spot, ↗ arrow, 24–48h, cross-platform arbitrage), and logging the sound per post for the §6 teardown. Open founder decisions live in that doc §7.
