# Mela Social Launch Playbook

**Last updated:** July 2026  
**Audience:** Arun (executor) + [Wife] (reviewer/eventual face of Mela)

---

## Why Cold Start Matters

You're starting with zero followers on a new account. The first month determines:
- **Does the aesthetic work?** (Are posts visually consistent?)
- **Does the curation resonate?** (Do people save/click posts about these brands?)
- **Is this sustainable?** (Can one person manage this long-term?)
- **Should [wife] take over?** (Does the account feel like her?)

Cold start isn't about viral growth. It's about **proving the model works so you can recruit your wife as the face of Mela.**

---

## Month 1 Timeline

### Week 1: Establish Consistency
- **Posts:** 5 (Mon–Fri)
- **Goal:** Show that Mela has a consistent, beautiful curation aesthetic
- **Your job:** Post Mon–Fri per schedule. Reply to comments same day (shows account is alive)
- **Wife's job:** See posts live, validate aesthetic ("this looks like Mela"), feel confident in the brand voice
- **Watch for:** Which post gets the most saves? Which brand resonates? (Data informs Week 2)

### Week 2: Test + Repeat
- **Posts:** 5 (same 5/week cadence)
- **Goal:** Lean into what worked Week 1; test something different if Week 1 was flat
- **Decision:** After 10 posts live, does wife think "yes, I could do this"?
- **Watch for:** Follower growth rate (should be 5–20/day organic)

### Week 3: Double Down or Pivot
- **Posts:** 5
- **Goal:** If clear winners exist, feature them more. If mixed, try different category/format.
- **Decision point:** Does wife want to take over posting + engagement starting Week 4?
- **Watch for:** Which brands/categories drive clicks to Mela site? (This is the real metric, not followers)

### Week 4: Stabilize or Test
- **Posts:** 5
- **Goal:** Establish a sustainable routine. If wife is taking over, transition to her driving content decisions.
- **Handoff:** If metrics look good + wife is in, she becomes the face (posts daily, responds, grows the audience)

## Success Metrics (Month 1 Target)

| Metric | Target | Reality Check |
|---|---|---|
| Followers | 300–500 organic | New account, organic-only (no paid ads) |
| Engagement rate | 3–8% saves+comments per post | Depends on product appeal, not followers |
| Clicks to Mela | 5–10 per post | Real win—followers who actually care |
| Wife buy-in | "Yes, I'll take over" | The real metric; everything else supports this |
| Aesthetic consistency | Wife nods (doesn't need tweaks) | Validates visual-style-guide execution |

## Wife Recruitment Strategy

**Why her?** She has better taste, more authentic social energy, and represents the Mela curation philosophy better than Arun-the-founder.

**How to convince her?**

1. **Month -1 (now):** Build proof of concept
   - Show her the visual-style-guide aesthetic
   - Let her review Week 1 posts before they go live
   - Get her input on which brands to feature ("does this feel like Mela?")

2. **Week 1 (posts live):** Demonstrate momentum
   - "Look, posts are going live consistently"
   - "First posts got [X saves], which is solid for a new account"
   - "I automated the creation, so you just post + engage"

3. **Week 2 (early traction):** Show she can scale it
   - "We have early followers. These are people who like the curation."
   - "The work is: post daily, reply to comments, follow 20 accounts/week. That's maybe 1 hour/day."
   - "You'd be the face, I'll handle content creation + backend"

4. **Week 3 (decision point):** Ask for commitment
   - "Metrics look good. Will you take over starting Week 4?"
   - Frame as: "This is the Mela brand. You're the curator. Own it."

**Key insight:** Don't ask for 10 hours/week of work. Ask for 1 hour/day of engagement + a 30-min weekly aesthetic review. That's sustainable.

## What to Watch Each Week

### Week 1
- ✓ Posts go live on schedule (consistency matters)
- ✓ Any post break 15+ saves (signal of resonance)
- ✓ Comments are positive (if any)
- ✗ Don't obsess over follower count (takes 2–3 weeks to build)

### Week 2
- ✓ Which brands/categories won Week 1? (double down)
- ✓ Follower growth trend (should be 5–15/day)
- ✓ Wife confidence ("I could do this")
- ✗ Don't launch Reels yet (master feed first)

### Week 3
- ✓ Clicks to Mela site (more important than followers)
- ✓ Which products converted? (data for brand)
- ✓ Wife ready to take over? (final decision)
- ✗ Don't pay for ads yet (organic is proving the model)

### Week 4
- ✓ Transition execution to wife (if she commits)
- ✓ Establish sustainable cadence (5 posts/week + 1 hour engagement)
- ✓ Prepare Month 2 strategy (growth vs. optimization)
- ✗ Don't expect viral (Month 1 is about consistency, not hype)

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Posting inconsistently.** If posts skip days, algorithm de-prioritizes you. Batch-create Week 1 all at once so you never miss a day.

❌ **Obsessing over followers.** A new account with 100 engaged followers is better than 1K disengaged ones. Track clicks + saves, not just follows.

❌ **Forcing wife into "social media manager" role.** She's the *curator*, not the operator. You create content, she approves + posts. Big difference.

❌ **Changing aesthetic mid-month.** Stick to visual-style-guide for all of Month 1. Let the aesthetic prove itself before tweaking.

❌ **Engaging with the wrong accounts.** Don't follow random accounts. Follow brands + complementary accounts in the artisan/sustainable space. Quality > quantity.

## FAQ

**Q: How many hours/week will this take?**  
A: Arun: 3–4 hours/week (batch creation once, scheduling). Wife (if she takes over): 1 hour/day (posting + engagement).

**Q: What if Week 1 gets zero engagement?**  
A: Pause and diagnose. Check: Are the visuals in-brand? Are captions resonating? Is the product selection on-brand? Adjust Week 2 and retest.

**Q: Should we pay for ads?**  
A: Not yet. Month 1 is about proving organic growth is possible. If organic stalls, ads might help, but data will tell.

**Q: What if wife says no?**  
A: The account can still work, but growth is slower (Arun posting is lower energy than [wife] curating). Plan B: hire a community manager who embodies Mela's taste.

**Q: How do we know cold-start is "over"?**  
A: When you hit 1K followers + wife is posting + you're seeing 5–10 clicks/day to Mela. Then Month 2 is about growth (more posts, more engagement, maybe paid ads).

## Handoff to Month 2

By end of Week 4, you'll have:
- A 30-day calendar + visual proof that the aesthetic works
- Early data on which brands/categories resonate
- Wife as the face (or a decision to hire someone else)
- A sustainable 5-posts/week cadence

Month 2 strategy: Growth (followers, engagement, conversion). Month 3+: Scale (partnerships, paid ads, TikTok/other platforms).

**For now: Execute Month 1. Prove the model. Recruit the team.**
